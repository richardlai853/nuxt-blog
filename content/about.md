# About

This starter is designed to provide a practical baseline for a production-ready blog app.

## Included capabilities

- Nuxt Content-driven posts under `content/posts`
- Filtering and search on the posts page
- Per-tag discovery pages
- Canonical metadata, sitemap and RSS endpoints
- Analytics ID placeholder via `NUXT_PUBLIC_ANALYTICS_ID`
